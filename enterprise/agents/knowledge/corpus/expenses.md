# Expenses

Submit out-of-pocket expenses in the Nimbus ledger within **30 days**.

- Meals are capped at **$75** per person unless a VP pre-approves.
- Expenses over **$200** require manager approval.
- Expenses over **$2,000** route to Finance.
- Expenses over **$10,000** route to the VP of the cost center.
- Personal items, gifts, and political donations are not reimbursable.
