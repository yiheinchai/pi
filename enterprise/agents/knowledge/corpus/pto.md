# Paid time off

Full-time Nimbus employees receive **20 days** of PTO each calendar year.

- Unused PTO may carry over a maximum of **5 days**.
- PTO requests of 3 or more consecutive days need manager approval in Workday.
- Parental leave is **16 weeks** of paid leave and does not consume PTO.
- Contractors should use the agreement in their SOW instead of this policy.
